# Project Guidelines

## Firm rules
- Match section numbering to the project instructions; add subsections when useful.
- Keep text taken from the project instructions in blue.
- Follow the notation used in the course materials as closely as the model allows; define any necessary deviations clearly and use the chosen notation consistently throughout the project.

## Soft preference
- Keep the small annotation text and preserve the established compact, consistent spacing between its lines.
- Prefer a concise, underlined equation description within the prose rather than a separate heading line. When related equations (such as modes 1 and 2) are presented together, group them under one equation number and refer to both in the prose.
